Tools that live in your terminal

Chat with friends, ask a whole garden of AI models at once, keep your files in a locked drive that sends them to any of your devices, let an AI tidy your Downloads folder (carefully), and learn to code in a cozy editor with a fox who explains things. Each one is a single command, and one more command installs them all and keeps them up to date.

Install Project CLI Access

It's a little menu: pick a project, press Enter, and it installs and opens it. It also keeps every project up to date by itself.

curl -fsSL {{origin}}/pca/install.sh | sh

Paste it into Terminal, then open a new window and type pca.

  • gossip v{{gossip_version}}

    End-to-end encrypted chat in the terminal: people, servers, calls and files. Pebble keeps watch.

    Open gossip

  • tree of thought v{{tot_version}}

    Many AI models in one garden. Set up free AI in a minute (copy a key, done), ask one model or all of them, and every answer becomes a branch you can go back to. Comes with a dashboard, a usage tracker, flashcards and terminal help.

    Open Tree of Thought

  • pigeon v{{pigeon_version}}

    Your own locked drive: folders, previews, share links and a trash, in the terminal or the browser. Send something to another device and it lands there by itself.

    Open pigeon

  • tidy v{{tidy_version}}

    Tell it how you want a folder tidied, in your own words. It reads names, file types and a little of what's inside, shows you every move first, never deletes anything, and one key puts it all back. Hazel the squirrel files things away.

    Open tidy

  • den v{{den_version}}

    A cozy code editor that opens in your browser: your files, a Run button, live previews of your web pages, and Ember the fox, who explains words and errors in plain English. Made for learning.

    Open den

  • pca v{{pca_version}}

    Project CLI Access: one menu that installs and opens everything above, and quietly keeps it all up to date.

    Open pca
