Get every app at once

Three steps, about a minute. After that, everything keeps itself up to date.

  1. Open Terminal

    Hold ⌘ and press space, type Terminal, press return.

  2. Copy the line below, paste it in, press Enter

    Press Copy, then click in the window that opened and paste.

  3. Type pca and press Enter

    Pick an app with the arrow keys and press Enter. It installs itself and opens.
