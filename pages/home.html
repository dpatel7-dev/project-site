Little apps you open by typing one word

Ask anything and get a clear answer. Chat with friends. Free up space. Tidy a messy folder. Learn to code. Each one is a word you type in Terminal, and one menu installs them all and keeps them up to date.

Get everything in 3 steps

1
Open Terminal

Hold ⌘ and press space, type Terminal, press return.

2
Copy this, paste it in, press Enter
curl -fsSL {{origin}}/pca/install.sh | sh
3
Type pca and press Enter

Use the arrow keys to pick an app, then press Enter. It installs itself and opens.
