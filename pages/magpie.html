Copy now, paste whenever.

Copied a link an hour ago, then copied something else? Press ⌃⌥V (control + option + V) in any app, type a word, press Enter, and it's back on your clipboard. Text, links, code, colours, pictures and files. Pica the magpie keeps the shiny things.

Get magpie

magpie works on Macs.

  1. Open Terminal (not sure how? the picture guide shows you).
  2. Copy this, paste it in, and press Enter:
    curl -fsSL {{origin}}/magpie/install.sh | sh
  3. Then type magpie and press Enter.

Already have pca? Open it and pick this card instead. Version {{magpie_version}}.

Using it

magpie onstart it once: from then on it keeps what you copy, also after a restart
⌃⌥Vin any app: the picker. Type to find, Enter copies it, then ⌘V. ⌘1 to ⌘9 pick one straight away
magpie appeverything you copied: search, filters, pictures, pins (or open Magpie from Spotlight)
magpiethe latest few in Terminal; type a number to copy it again
magpie pin 3keep something forever
magpie copy 2 --as urlcopy it back changed: a link without trackers, tidy spaces, UPPERCASE, pretty JSON…
magpie pause 1hstop for a while
magpie updateget the newest magpie

Your secrets stay secret

  • Anything a password manager marks as secret is never read, and password managers are ignored from the start.
  • Things that look like passwords, API keys, tokens or card numbers aren't kept.
  • Nothing leaves your computer, unless you send something to your other devices with pigeon yourself.
  • No administrator needed. It changes nothing on your Mac except its own key combo. If the key combo doesn't work straight away, log out and in once, or press ⌘Space and type picker.
  • Mac only.
