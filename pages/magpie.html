Using it

magpie on
start it once: from then on it keeps what you copy, also after a restart
⌃⌥V
in any app: the picker. Type to find, Enter copies it, then ⌘V. ⌘1 to ⌘9 pick one straight away
magpie app
everything you copied: search, filters, pictures, pins (or open Magpie from Spotlight)
magpie
the latest few in Terminal; type a number to copy it again
magpie pin 3
keep something forever
magpie copy 2 --as url
copy it back changed: a link without trackers, tidy spaces, UPPERCASE, pretty JSON…
magpie pause 1h
stop for a while
magpie update
get the newest magpie

Your secrets stay secret

  • Anything a password manager marks as secret is never read, and password managers are ignored from the start.
  • Things that look like passwords, API keys, tokens or card numbers aren't kept.
  • Nothing leaves your computer, unless you send something to your other devices with pigeon yourself.
  • No administrator needed. It changes nothing on your Mac except its own key combo. If the key combo doesn't work straight away, log out and in once, or press ⌘Space and type picker.
  • Mac only.
