Ask every model. Keep every answer.

Tree of Thought is a terminal chat for AI models. Plant the ones you like in your garden, ask one of them or all of them at once, and every answer grows into a branch you can come back to.

curl -fsSL {{origin}}/tot/install.sh | sh

Paste it into Terminal, then open a new window and type tot. Version {{tot_version}}.

Every answer is a branch

Ctrl+G sends your question to the whole garden, and ← → flips between the answers. Edit a question and the old conversation stays right where it was.

Every model gets a plant

Your models line up down the left side of the screen, each with its own little pixel plant, picked by the model's name.

    Plant your first models

    You need a key from at least one AI service. It stays on your computer, readable only by you.

    1. Install it

      Paste the command at the top of this page into your terminal. If Node.js isn't on your computer, the installer brings its own private copy.

    2. Get a key

      The easy one is OpenRouter: one key reaches Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Llama, Mistral, Qwen and hundreds more.

      Free options: Google AI Studio, Groq, or Ollama on your own computer.

    3. Paste it in

      Type tot, press Tab, choose API keys, and paste. Your garden fills with the strongest recent model from each maker.

    Keys

    Enter
    Ask. Alt+Enter for a new line.
    Ctrl+G
    Ask every model in your garden at once.
    ← →
    Flip between branches.
    Ctrl+E
    Edit your last question into a new branch.
    Ctrl+R
    Ask the current model again.
    Ctrl+Y
    Copy the last answer.
    ↑ ↓
    Pick a model or a chat.
    Tab
    Models, keys, colours and chats.
