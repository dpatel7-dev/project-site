medic

Why is my Mac so slow?

medic looks at your Mac the way Activity Monitor does, then tells you what it means in plain words: the biggest problem first, and a button for the fix when there's a safe one. Memory, heat, battery, disk, Wi-Fi, what starts by itself. Lotl the axolotl does the checking; axolotls are famous for healing themselves.

2 things need attention. The biggest: your Mac is short on memory. After that: it's been 15 days since your Mac restarted.

Your Mac is short on memory

It has 8 GB and needs more right now, so it's using the disk as extra memory (4.9 GB). That's the most common reason a Mac feels slow. Using the most: Google Chrome 3.4 GB.

The battery holds 86% of what it did when new

That's normal wear. Keeping it between 20% and 80% most of the time helps it last.

Get medic

medic works on Macs.

  1. Open Terminal (not sure how? the picture guide shows you).
  2. Copy this, paste it in, and press Enter:
    curl -fsSL {{origin}}/medic/install.sh | sh
  3. Then type medic and press Enter.

Already have pca? Open it and pick this card instead. Version {{medic_version}}.

Using it

medica quick checkup; type a number to do one of the fixes
medic checkthe full checkup: Apple's battery figures, Wi-Fi, how fast the internet answers
medic appthe window: everything at once, with fix buttons and a pond at the bottom (or open medic from Spotlight)
medic batteryhealth, charge cycles, temperature
medic hogswhat's using the most power and memory
medic startupwhat starts by itself; medic startup off 2 turns one off
medic diary ona note every 5 minutes, then medic diary shows what drains the battery

Safe fixes only

  • medic only reads, unless you press a button.
  • Quitting an app is like choosing Quit yourself: it asks you to save first. medic never quits parts of macOS.
  • It only turns off your own startup helpers, and puts them aside instead of deleting them, so they come back with one click.
  • No administrator needed, and it never changes a Mac's settings, so it's fine on a school Mac.
  • Nothing leaves your computer. Mac only.
