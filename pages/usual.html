Friday pizza, two clicks.

Type your name, phone, email and address once. Save your usual orders (who each thing is for, too) and usual prints each one as a receipt with the total and what everyone owes. Then it helps you order: every detail ready to copy into the website, exactly what to say on the phone, or the whole order written out to email. Rocco the raccoon knows where the good food is.

Using it

usual
open the window: it walks you through setting up (you, a place, your usual)
Order online
opens the place's website, with your name, phone, email, address and the order each one click from being copied
Fill in my details
a bookmark that fills in checkout pages for you (in You)
Call
the number, and exactly what to say
I ordered it
stamps the receipt and keeps it in your history, with what you spend each month
usual order pizza
the same, in Terminal
usual on
once: reminders like "Friday pizza?", and usual in Spotlight
usual lock on
ask for Touch ID before showing anything

Your details stay yours

  • Everything is saved on your computer only, in ~/.usual. usual never sends your details anywhere: you choose when to paste or send them.
  • usual never asks for card numbers. You pay the place the way you always do.
  • The Touch ID lock is Apple's own check, so a photo can't fool it. (Mac only.)
  • The checkout filler only fills boxes it recognises, skips password boxes, and never sends anything anywhere. Check what it filled before you order.
  • Works on Mac, Windows and Linux.
