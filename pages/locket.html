locket

One password to remember.

locket keeps all your passwords locked up with one master password (or Touch ID), and makes them easy to use: copy one with a click, make strong new ones, see 2-step codes, and find out which passwords are weak, used twice, or leaked. Dilly the armadillo walks about the desert while it's unlocked, and curls up into a ball when it locks.

Get locket

  1. Open Terminal (not sure how? the picture guide shows you).
  2. Copy this, paste it in, and press Enter:
    curl -fsSL {{origin}}/locket/install.sh | sh
  3. Then type locket and press Enter.

Already have pca? Open it and pick this card instead. Version {{locket_version}}.

Using it

locketopen locket and make your vault (your master password: long, used nowhere else, written down somewhere safe at home)
Bring passwords instep by step for Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Bitwarden, 1Password and LastPass
Copya username or password with one click. Copied passwords clear themselves after 30 seconds
Make one for mea strong new password: random characters, or words that are easy to type
Check-upleaked, used more than once, or easy to guess
locket ononce: an armadillo in the menu bar and ⌃⌥L for a side panel, for quick copying
locket get githubcopy a password from Terminal
locket makea strong password, from Terminal

How your passwords are kept safe

  • Your master password goes through scrypt (slow on purpose, so guessing takes a very long time), which unlocks a random key that locks your passwords with AES-256-GCM. Your master password is never saved anywhere.
  • If you forget your master password, nobody can get your passwords back, not even locket. Write it down and keep it somewhere safe.
  • Touch ID is Apple's own check, so a photo or a video can't fool it. (Mac only.)
  • The leak check (Have I Been Pwned) never sends your passwords: only the first 5 characters of each one's fingerprint leave your computer.
  • Copied passwords are marked secret, so clipboard keepers like magpie skip them.
  • Your vault stays on your computer, in ~/.locket. Works on Mac, Windows and Linux.
