Sorts a messy folder for you. Carefully.

Pick a folder and a plan is ready at once: installers together, pictures together, documents together. You see every move before it makes one, one key moves them, and one key puts it all back. Want something cleverer, like "by school subject"? Ask the AI, in your own words.

    How it stays careful

    The AI (and the instant plans) only suggest. tidy checks every single suggestion itself and drops anything that breaks a rule, so even a confused AI can't make a mess.

    • Plans first

      Nothing moves until you've seen the whole plan, skipped what you don't like, and pressed Enter.

    • Only moves

      Never deletes, renames or edits a file. If a name is already taken where something's going, it gets a (2): nothing is replaced.

    • Stays inside

      Only the folder you pick. It refuses your whole disk, your whole home folder and system folders, and never follows a link out.

    • Keeps private things private

      Passwords, keys, tax forms and bank statements are never opened; the AI sees their names only. Other snippets have secrets blanked out.

    • Keeps things whole

      Projects (with .git or package.json), apps and hidden files stay as they are, and nothing is ever moved into a project.

    • Undo always works

      Every move is written down before it happens, so tidy undo works even if your computer switched off halfway through.

    Get tidy

    The easy way: open pca and pick tidy. Or paste this:

    curl -fsSL {{origin}}/tidy/install.sh | sh
    1. Paste it into Terminal and press Enter.
    2. Type tidy, pick a folder, press Enter. Sorting by kind or by date needs no AI at all.
    3. To ask the AI too: set up free AI once with tot free (from Tree of Thought), or install Ollama so nothing leaves your computer. tidy asks once before a cloud AI sees any file names.
