An AI that tidies a folder. Carefully.

Tell it what you want in your own words. tidy reads the names, the kinds of files, a little of what's inside, and how your folders are already set up. Then it shows you every move before it makes one, never deletes anything, and one key puts it all back.

    How it stays careful

    The AI only suggests. tidy checks every single suggestion itself and drops anything that breaks a rule, so even a confused AI can't make a mess.

    • Plans first

      Nothing moves until you've seen the whole plan, switched off what you don't like, and pressed Enter twice.

    • Only moves

      Never deletes, renames or edits a file. If a name is already taken where something's going, it gets a (2): nothing is replaced.

    • Stays inside

      Only the folder you pick. It refuses your whole disk, your whole home folder and system folders, and never follows a link out.

    • Keeps private things private

      Passwords, keys, tax forms and bank statements are never opened; the AI sees their names only. Other snippets have secrets blanked out.

    • Keeps things whole

      Projects (with .git or package.json), apps and hidden files stay as they are, and nothing is ever moved into a project.

    • Undo always works

      Every move is written down before it happens, so tidy undo works even if your computer switched off halfway through.

    Get tidy

    The easy way: open pca and pick tidy. Or paste this:

    curl -fsSL {{origin}}/tidy/install.sh | sh
    1. Paste it into Terminal and press Enter.
    2. tidy needs an AI. The private way: install Ollama, then tot free or ollama pull llama3.2. Nothing about your files leaves your computer. (It can also use Tree of Thought's models; it asks before sending anything.)
    3. Type tidy ~/Downloads.
