scout

Ask anything. Get a clear answer.

scout searches the web for you, reads the best pages at the same time, and writes a short answer in plain words. Every fact has a little number: click it to see the website it came from. Pip the owl does the reading.

Get scout

  1. Open Terminal (not sure how? the picture guide shows you).
  2. Copy this, paste it in, and press Enter:
    curl -fsSL {{origin}}/scout/install.sh | sh
  3. Then type scout and press Enter.

Already have pca? Open it and pick this card instead. Version {{scout_version}}.

Using it

scoutopens scout in a window: type a question and press Enter
scout why is the sky bluethe answer right in Terminal, with its sources
Click a numberopens the website that fact came from
Settings (the gear)set up free AI, pick who answers, clear your past questions
scout updateget the newest scout

Good to know

  • The first time, scout asks for a free AI key. Pick Groq, copy the key, paste it in: about a minute. If you use Tree of Thought, tidy, den or hey, it's already set up.
  • It searches with DuckDuckGo and Wikipedia, no account needed. A free Brave Search key (in Settings) makes results steadier.
  • Your past questions stay on your computer, and you can clear them any time.
  • scout tells you when the websites disagree, or when it couldn't find the answer.
