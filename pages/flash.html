Good to know

  • Making cards with AI needs a free key, once. It's shared with Tree of Thought, den, scout and hey, so if you set one up there, it's already here. Everything else works without AI.
  • Read AI-made cards over: AI can get things wrong. Click a card to fix it.
  • Typing answers forgives small typos, accents and "the" in front, and accepts alternatives like colour / color.
  • Your decks stay on your computer in ~/.flash. Export them any time for Quizlet or Anki.
  • Works on Mac, Windows and Linux. Reminders and reading PDFs are Mac only.
