morph

Turn any file into any other.

A video, and you only want the sound? A photo your computer won't open? A Word file that has to be a PDF? morph converts it right on your computer: about 2,000 conversions between 127 formats, plus tools to merge, split and shrink PDFs. Luma the jellyfish drifts along while it works.

Get morph

  1. Open Terminal (not sure how? the picture guide shows you).
  2. Copy this, paste it in, and press Enter:
    curl -fsSL {{origin}}/morph/install.sh | sh
  3. Then type morph and press Enter.

Already have pca? Open it and pick this card instead. Version {{morph_version}}.

Using it

morphopens morph in its own window: drop files in, pick a format, click Convert
Choose filesor drag them in, or paste one with ⌘V
PDF toolsmerge, split, shrink or rotate PDFs, or turn photos into one PDF
morph updateget the newest morph
morph repairset it up again if it won't start
Ctrl+Cin the Terminal window: closes morph

Good to know

  • morph needs Python 3. On a Mac, if you don't have it: type xcode-select --install in Terminal and click Install.
  • The first start takes a few minutes while morph installs its converters. After that it opens in seconds.
  • Your files never leave your computer. Converted files are deleted after an hour.
  • Keep the Terminal window open while you convert; closing it closes morph.
