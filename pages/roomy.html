roomy

Make room on your computer, safely.

Your disk is full and you don't know why. roomy shows you, ticks the easy things to clear, and clears them with one key. Everything goes to the Trash first, so you can change your mind. Shelly the hermit crab knows all about moving somewhere roomier.

Get roomy

  1. Open Terminal (not sure how? the picture guide shows you).
  2. Copy this, paste it in, and press Enter:
    curl -fsSL {{origin}}/roomy/install.sh | sh
  3. Then type roomy and press Enter.

Already have pca? Open it and pick this card instead. Version {{roomy_version}}.

Using it

roomyopen it: see what's using space, tick what to clear, press Enter
Spacetick or untick a row

→Biggest folders: look inside what takes the most space
roomy checkjust list what could be cleared, changing nothing

How it stays safe

  • Everything goes to the Trash first, and u (or roomy putback) puts it back.
  • Emptying the Trash is the only thing that can't be undone, so it's never ticked for you and roomy asks first.
  • It only clears things that come back by themselves (caches, build files, package downloads) or that you threw away.
  • It only looks inside your home folder and never follows links somewhere else.
