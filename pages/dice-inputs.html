Inputs, step by step

DiceOS can switch your TV or your computer screen to the other things plugged into it, and back again, and share its keyboard and mouse with another computer. That's a KVM switch, without buying one. Pick what you want to do.

If something doesn't work

The TV doesn't switch, or Inputs says the TV isn't answering

HDMI-CEC has to be on in the TV's settings (Samsung: Anynet+), and on in the thing you're switching to. Try a different HDMI cable or port: some old cables don't carry it. On a Raspberry Pi, use the HDMI port nearest the power socket.

My PlayStation (or Xbox) isn't in the list

Turn it on first, then press Look again in Inputs. Check its HDMI-CEC setting (PlayStation 5: Enable HDMI Device Link). Even if it never shows up by name, its HDMI port is still there to pick.

No screen shows up on my PC

Turn on DDC/CI in the screen's own menu, and plug straight into the screen (not through a dock or an adapter). A laptop's own screen can't switch. If DiceOS is older than 2.2, open Terminal and type sudo dice-update.

The screen switched away and I can't get back

The keyboard is still plugged into the DiceOS computer: press Esc or Home on it and the screen switches back. If your screen doesn't listen while it's showing the other computer, press the screen's own input button once.

Deskflow: the pointer doesn't move to the other computer

Both computers need to be on the same network. The name you gave the other screen in Deskflow on DiceOS must match the other computer's name exactly. On a Mac, allow Deskflow in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Accessibility.

Can Deuce do it?

Yes: "switch to the PlayStation", "switch to HDMI 2", "switch the screen to displayport", or a name you gave one, like "switch to the work laptop". "Back to Dice" comes back.
