hum

Your music, off to one side.

hum floats at the edge of your screen, over whatever you're doing, without taking it over. Click the bee in the menu bar (or press ⌃⌥M) to open and close it. Your own music files, songs from YouTube, playlists and a queue. Bumble the bumblebee flies along the bar as the song plays, and the flowers in the garden at the bottom dance to your music: they rock on the beat, bob, hop, sparkle on the high notes, doze off when it stops and take a bow at the end. Press M and it all shrinks into a little player in the corner.

Using it

hum on
once: a bee in your menu bar, ⌃⌥M for the panel, ready every time you log in
hum add ~/Music
add your music files (mp3, m4a, wav, aiff, flac). Or the + in the panel
hum add https://youtu.be/…
a song from YouTube: it plays in YouTube's own player, in the panel
hum
what's playing, with keys to control it right in Terminal
hum play homework
play a playlist (or a song)
hum pause · next · prev
from Terminal
M
in the panel: hum becomes a little player tucked in the bottom right corner (or hum small). M again, or hum big, makes it big
hum app
hum in a bigger window
hum doctor
check every part, if something doesn't work

Good to know

  • hum never downloads anything from YouTube or turns videos into MP3s: that's against YouTube's rules. YouTube songs play in YouTube's own player, and pause when you close the panel.
  • Your music files stay where they are; hum only remembers where.
  • Songs from Apple Music are locked to the Music app, so hum skips those.
  • No administrator needed. The panel and the menu bar bee are Mac only; elsewhere hum opens in a window.
