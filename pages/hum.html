hum

Your music, off to one side.

hum floats at the edge of your screen, over whatever you're doing, without taking it over. Click the bee in the menu bar (or press ⌃⌥M) to open and close it. Your own music files, songs from YouTube, playlists and a queue. Bumble the bumblebee flies along the bar as the song plays, and the garden at the bottom sways to the music.

Get hum

  1. Open Terminal (not sure how? the picture guide shows you).
  2. Copy this, paste it in, and press Enter:
    curl -fsSL {{origin}}/hum/install.sh | sh
  3. Then type hum and press Enter.

Already have pca? Open it and pick this card instead. Version {{hum_version}}.

Using it

hum ononce: a bee in your menu bar, ⌃⌥M for the panel, ready every time you log in
hum add ~/Musicadd your music files (mp3, m4a, wav, aiff, flac). Or the + in the panel
hum add https://youtu.be/…a song from YouTube: it plays in YouTube's own player, in the panel
humwhat's playing, with keys to control it right in Terminal
hum play homeworkplay a playlist (or a song)
hum pause · next · prevfrom Terminal
hum apphum in a bigger window
hum doctorcheck every part, if something doesn't work

Good to know

  • hum never downloads anything from YouTube or turns videos into MP3s: that's against YouTube's rules. YouTube songs play in YouTube's own player, and pause when you close the panel.
  • Your music files stay where they are; hum only remembers where.
  • Songs from Apple Music are locked to the Music app, so hum skips those.
  • No administrator needed. The panel and the menu bar bee are Mac only; elsewhere hum opens in a window.
