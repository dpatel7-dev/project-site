shoo

Ads, shooed off your whole Mac.

Google's full-page ads that cover the page, the banners, the pop-ups, the trackers that follow you around, and the ads on YouTube. shoo blocks them for every browser and app on your Mac. It's an app, not an extension, and Bramble the hedgehog keeps watch from a little pixel forest.

Today Bramble kept 23 ads out of YouTube, and 128,431 ad and tracker addresses are blocked on this Mac.

Get shoo

shoo works on Macs.

  1. Open Terminal (not sure how? the picture guide shows you).
  2. Copy this, paste it in, and press Enter:
    curl -fsSL {{origin}}/shoo/install.sh | sh
  3. Then type shoo and press Enter.

Already have pca? Open it and pick this card instead. Version {{shoo_version}}.

Using it

shooopens shoo's window (the first time, it sets itself up)
shoo offturns it off and puts every setting back
shoo allow example.comlets a site through if blocking it broke something
shoo statuswhat shoo is doing
shoo listgets the newest list of ad addresses
shoo updategets the newest shoo

Safe by design

  • An administrator of the Mac approves it once, in macOS's own password box. On a family Mac, that's whoever owns it.
  • It won't change a Mac a school or company manages, and it won't go around a filter that's already set up.
  • Only YouTube passes through shoo; everything else goes straight to the internet. If shoo stops, YouTube still works.
  • Its certificate works for YouTube only and never leaves your Mac. shoo off puts everything back.
  • Mac only for now. No ad blocker catches every single ad: YouTube changes often, so update shoo now and then.
