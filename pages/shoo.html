Using it

shoo
opens shoo's window (the first time, it sets itself up). The shoo app in Applications does the same
shoo chrome
opens Chrome with shoo (the Chrome with shoo app does too)
shoo off
turns it off
shoo allow example.com
lets a site through if blocking it broke something
shoo status
what shoo is doing
shoo list
gets the newest list of ad addresses
shoo update
gets the newest shoo

Safe by design

  • No administrator needed for Chrome, Edge or Brave. macOS may ask for your own password once. Every app and Safari needs an administrator to approve it.
  • On a Mac a school manages, it never touches the Mac's settings: it works only inside your browser, and the school's filters and rules stay as they are.
  • If shoo stops, your browser just goes straight to every site, so nothing breaks.
  • Its certificate works for YouTube only and never leaves your Mac. shoo uninstall removes everything.
  • Mac only for now. No ad blocker catches every single ad: YouTube changes often, so update shoo now and then.
