shoo

Ads, shooed away. No admin needed.

Google's full-page ads that cover the page, the banners, the pop-ups, the trackers that follow you around, and the ads on YouTube. shoo blocks them in Chrome, Edge or Brave, with no administrator needed, and in every app and Safari too once an administrator approves it. It's an app, not an extension, and Bramble the hedgehog keeps watch from a little hedgerow.

Today Bramble kept 23 ads out of YouTube, and stopped 412 ads and trackers on websites.

Get shoo

shoo works on Macs.

  1. Open Terminal (not sure how? the picture guide shows you).
  2. Copy this, paste it in, and press Enter:
    curl -fsSL {{origin}}/shoo/install.sh | sh
  3. Then type shoo and press Enter.

Already have pca? Open it and pick this card instead. Version {{shoo_version}}.

Using it

shooopens shoo's window (the first time, it sets itself up). The shoo app in Applications does the same
shoo chromeopens Chrome with shoo (the Chrome with shoo app does too)
shoo offturns it off
shoo allow example.comlets a site through if blocking it broke something
shoo statuswhat shoo is doing
shoo listgets the newest list of ad addresses
shoo updategets the newest shoo

Safe by design

  • No administrator needed for Chrome, Edge or Brave. macOS may ask for your own password once. Every app and Safari needs an administrator to approve it.
  • On a Mac a school manages, it never touches the Mac's settings: it works only inside your browser, and the school's filters and rules stay as they are.
  • If shoo stops, your browser just goes straight to every site, so nothing breaks.
  • Its certificate works for YouTube only and never leaves your Mac. shoo uninstall removes everything.
  • Mac only for now. No ad blocker catches every single ad: YouTube changes often, so update shoo now and then.
