Chat with your friends, right in the terminal.

gossip is a small chat app for direct messages and group servers. Only the people in a chat can read it, and it runs on Mac, Windows and Linux.

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dpatel7-dev/gossip/main/install.sh | sh

Paste it into Terminal and press Enter. Then open a new window and type gossip.

From nothing to chatting in a minute

You don't need an account anywhere else, and there's nothing to sign up for.

  1. Paste the install lineIt's at the top of this page. It sets everything up, including Node.js if your computer doesn't have it.
  2. Type gossipOpen a new terminal window first, so it knows the new command.
  3. Pick a usernameYour secret keys are made on your computer and never leave it.
  4. Add a friendPress Tab for the menu, choose "Add a person", and say hi.

A whole chat app, and nothing to memorise

gossip opens as a proper chat screen: your people and servers on the left, the conversation on the right, a box to type in.

A menu does the remembering

Press Tab and everything is there: add a person, start a server, invite someone, change the colours. Arrow keys to move, Enter to choose. The mouse works as well.

Works when the internet doesn't

Run gossip nearby and it finds everyone else on the same Wi-Fi router or network cable. The router doesn't need to be online. If the person hosting leaves, someone else takes over without anyone noticing.

Private by default
Messages are locked on your computer and unlocked on your friend's, the same idea as iMessage. Whatever passes them along can see who is talking, never what is said.
Servers with an owner
Make a group open to everyone or invite-only. The owner can remove people, even when they're offline, and can hand the server to someone else.
It tells you, quietly
A pop-up from your computer when a message arrives and you're looking elsewhere. Mute any chat you'd like left alone.
Find it again
Search everything you've been sent with gossip find, or save a whole chat to a text file with gossip export.
Watch YouTube together
/yt and a link plays it in gossip's own player. /watch keeps everyone in the chat on the same moment: anyone pauses or skips, and it happens for all of you.
Calls that open in your browser
Voice or video with /call. Chat and send reactions during the call, or pop it out into a small window that stays on top while you work.
Say it later
/later 8am happy birthday! sends a message when it's due, and /remind 10m stretch taps you on the shoulder.
Do not disturb
/dnd 1h silences sounds and pop-ups for a while. Messages still arrive, quietly.
Looks after itself
gossip fetches new versions in the background and switches over the next time you open it.
Leaves cleanly
gossip uninstall removes it, and asks whether to keep your account for later.

Every menu item has a command, and they work from any terminal without opening the chat.

gossip
Open the chat.
gossip check
See what you missed.
gossip listen
Stay tuned and print messages as they arrive.

Send a message without opening anything.
gossip nearby
Chat with people on the same Wi-Fi or cable, no internet needed.
gossip config
Change the colours, the penguin, the layout and more.

What's new

The last few versions. In the app, gossip changelog shows the same list.

Version 0.10.0

  • A YouTube player, built in. Inside a chat, type /yt and paste a YouTube link: it plays in gossip's own player window, and the bottom line of the chat shows what's playing. /yt pause, /yt play, /yt +30 and /yt -10 control it from the chat.
  • YouTube links in messages get a ▶ YouTube video line under them. /yt plays the newest one in the chat, /videos lists them all, and /yt 2 plays the second.
  • Watch together: open a chat (a friend or a server), type /watch and a link. When the others type /watch too, you're in sync: anyone presses play, pauses or skips, and it happens for everyone. /watch stop leaves. Play and pause travel end-to-end encrypted, only to the people watching, and are never saved.
  • The player lists every video from your chats (with titles and pictures), lets you paste links, start watching together and send the video you're on to the chat. Keys: k or space, j/l, f, m.
  • The dashboard's conversation box has a "▶ play" button next to YouTube links.

Version 0.9.1

  • The penguin keeps its look: on macOS Terminal, "auto" picks the blocky penguin once and keeps it. Before, it switched to the detailed one when you started typing (that's when macOS Terminal reports its colours) and never switched back. Choose the detailed one any time in settings (mascot.style smooth).

Version 0.9.0

  • Suggestions while you type in the chat screen: start with / for commands, @ for people or # for servers. ↑ ↓ choose, Tab completes, Enter runs or opens it, Esc hides the list.
  • Each chat keeps its own half-typed message. Switch to another chat and back, and it's still there - and it never gets sent to the wrong person.
  • Ctrl+N jumps to the next chat with unread messages (the bottom line tells you when there are some).
  • Scrolled up? The bottom line tells you when new messages arrive below.
  • The dashboard has a Conversation box: click any chat - people or servers - to read it and reply right on the page. It updates live, shows when they're typing, and links are clickable.
