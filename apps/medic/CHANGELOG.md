# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- **Light or dark, set once for every app:** press **L** in pca (or `pca look light`, `pca look dark`, `pca look auto`). medic's window follows it.

## 1.0.1
- The moving scene behind the window can no longer stumble on its very first frame.

## 1.0.0
- medic is a checkup for your Mac: why it's slow, hot or low on battery, in plain words, with the biggest problem first.
- Looks at memory (and the disk standing in for memory), heat and slowing down to cool off, apps working hard, what's keeping the Mac awake, the battery's health, cycles and temperature, disk space, days since a restart, Wi-Fi and the internet, and what starts by itself.
- Safe fixes, nothing that needs an administrator: quit an app (it asks you to save first), turn off your own startup helpers (put aside, never deleted), open the right page of System Settings, or open roomy to find what's taking space.
- The battery diary: a note every five minutes, then how fast it drains, how long a full charge lasts, and which apps drained it.
- A note of the main figures each day, to see how things change (battery health over months, for example).
- `medic app` opens its window: the verdict, the findings with fix buttons, the details, the diary chart. At the bottom is a pond where Lotl the axolotl walks along the sand, rests and swims up for air. Sunbeams by day, glowing specks and moonlight at night.
