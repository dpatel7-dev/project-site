# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- hey is born. Say what you want (`hey find big files in my downloads`) and get the command, what it
  does in plain words, and how risky it is. Enter runs it; c copies it; e explains each part; t tells
  it what to change.
- `hey explain <command>`: what any command does, piece by piece.
- Its own risk rules: deleting, sudo, scripts from the internet, wiping disks and git resets are
  always **Risky** (type `yes` to run); moving, making and installing things are **Careful** (asks
  y/n), whatever the AI says.
- `hey setup`: free AI in about a minute, shared with Tree of Thought, tidy, den and scout.
