# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Morph joins the family: install it with one line (or from pca) and type `morph`. It sets up its own private Python the first time, starts on your computer and opens in a window of its own. `morph update`, `morph repair` and `morph uninstall` do what they say.
- Meet Luma, Morph's jellyfish. She drifts above the converter, blinks now and then, and is the new logo and tab icon.
- New colours: deep-sea ink buttons by day, Luma's glow by night, with a light/dark switch in the top corner (it follows your computer until you pick one).
