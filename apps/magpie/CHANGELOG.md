# Changelog

## 1.2.0
- **Light or dark, set once for every app:** press **L** in pca (or `pca look light`, `pca look dark`, `pca look auto`). The picker panel and the window follow it.

## 1.1.0
- The moving scene behind the window can no longer stumble on its very first frame.
- The picker is now a side panel: a narrow strip of frosted glass at the right of the screen that floats over your work without taking it over. The app you were in stays the active one.
- A magpie in the menu bar (top right of the screen): click it to open or close the panel. It always works, even when a key combo doesn't.
- Everything works from the panel: copy, copy changed (tidy spaces, UPPERCASE, a link without trackers…, each with a preview), pin, name, open a link, send to your devices, forget. New copies show up while it's open.
- ⌃⌥V is now claimed by magpie itself, without needing to log out first (the old macOS service stays as a spare).
- `magpie` on its own now stays open: type to find, arrows to choose, Enter copies, Tab opens the side panel, Esc leaves. Before, it printed a list and stopped straight away.
- `magpie doctor` checks every part and says plainly what works and what doesn't.

## 1.0.0
- magpie remembers everything you copy: text, links, code, colours, pictures and files, with the app it came from.
- Press **⌃⌥V** (control + option + V) in any app for the picker: type to find, Enter to copy, and you're back where you were. ⌘1–9 picks one straight away. Or ⌘Space › "picker".
- Skips passwords: anything a password manager marks as secret is never read, password-manager apps are ignored, and things that look like passwords, API keys, tokens or card numbers aren't kept.
- Repeats move to the top instead of piling up. Pin things to keep them forever, and give them names.
- Quick changes when you copy something back: clean a link of trackers, tidy spaces, UPPER/lower/Title Case, one line, sort, remove repeated lines, bullet list, pretty JSON, Base64, file-name-style.
- Pick several and copy them together (one per line, with blank lines, spaces or commas between).
- Send text to your other devices with pigeon, if you have it.
- Its window has rooftops at the bottom: Pica the magpie hops along them collecting shiny things. Windows light up at night.
- Nothing leaves your computer. No administrator needed.
