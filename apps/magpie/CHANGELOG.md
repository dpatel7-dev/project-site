# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- magpie remembers everything you copy: text, links, code, colours, pictures and files, with the app it came from.
- Press **⌃⌥V** (control + option + V) in any app for the picker: type to find, Enter to copy, and you're back where you were. ⌘1–9 picks one straight away. Or ⌘Space › "picker".
- Skips passwords: anything a password manager marks as secret is never read, password-manager apps are ignored, and things that look like passwords, API keys, tokens or card numbers aren't kept.
- Repeats move to the top instead of piling up. Pin things to keep them forever, and give them names.
- Quick changes when you copy something back: clean a link of trackers, tidy spaces, UPPER/lower/Title Case, one line, sort, remove repeated lines, bullet list, pretty JSON, Base64, file-name-style.
- Pick several and copy them together (one per line, with blank lines, spaces or commas between).
- Send text to your other devices with pigeon, if you have it.
- Its window has rooftops at the bottom: Pica the magpie hops along them collecting shiny things. Windows light up at night.
- Nothing leaves your computer. No administrator needed.
